## Billing, refunds & cancellation

We do not process payments, so we cannot issue refunds or see your billing details.
All subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google.

**Cancel a subscription**

- **iPhone / iPad:** Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → LucidSkill → Cancel Subscription
- **Android:** Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → LucidSkill → Cancel

Cancelling at least 24 hours before the period ends stops the next renewal. You keep access
until the current period runs out. Deleting the app does **not** cancel a subscription.

**Request a refund**

- **Apple:** reportaproblem.apple.com
- **Google:** Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & order history → select the order

**Restore a purchase** — already subscribed but the app is locked? Open the unlock screen
and tap **Restore purchases**, signed in with the same store account you bought with.

## Common questions

**I can't hear anything.** Check your device isn't on silent, raise the media volume, and
confirm the layer sliders in session tuning aren't all at zero. Headphones are recommended,
and are required for binaural tracks to work as intended.

**The flashing light is too intense.** Turn Lucid Flash off in session tuning, or lower its
intensity. If you have epilepsy, a seizure disorder, or are photosensitive, leave it off —
see the [Safety Notice](safety-and-disclaimer.md).

**My voice recording clicks or pops when it loops.** Re-record leaving a beat of silence at
the start and end, and keep an even volume through the phrase.

**Where did my created skills go?** They live only on your device. Deleting the app,
clearing its data, or using 'Delete all data' in Settings removes them permanently — there
is no cloud backup to restore from.

**How do I delete my data?** Settings → Delete all data. That erases everything the app
stores about you on that device.

## Safety

If you feel unwell during a session — headache, dizziness, nausea, altered vision, racing
heart, disorientation, or any involuntary movement — stop immediately. Read the full
[Safety Notice & Legal Disclaimer](safety-and-disclaimer.md) before use. Never use
LucidSkill while driving, in or near water, at height, or during any activity that needs
your attention.

LucidSkill is an educational and entertainment product. It is not a medical device and does
not provide medical advice. If you are in crisis or need urgent medical help, contact your
local emergency services.
