## Part 1 — Safety Notice

### Flashing lights & seizure risk

Sessions can include a rapidly flashing light overlay (roughly 4–40 flashes per second) and flickering video. A small percentage of people may experience seizures, blackouts or altered awareness when exposed to flashing lights or patterns, even with no prior history of epilepsy. Do NOT use the Lucid Flash feature if you have epilepsy, a seizure disorder, or are photosensitive. Stop the session immediately and consult a doctor if you experience dizziness, altered vision, eye or muscle twitching, loss of awareness, disorientation, or any involuntary movement.

### Never use while doing anything else

Sessions are immersive and designed to reduce your awareness of your surroundings. Only use LucidSkill while seated or lying down in a safe, private place. NEVER use it while driving, riding, cycling, or operating any vehicle or machinery; while in or near water, including a bath, pool, or open water; at height, on stairs, or near traffic, heat, flames or sharp objects; or while walking or exercising. Doing so can cause serious injury or death to you or others.

### Skills can be dangerous

Many skills in this app — flips, tricking, powermoves, acrobatics, martial arts, and similar — are inherently dangerous and can cause serious injury, paralysis or death. Mental rehearsal is NOT physical readiness: feeling ready inside a session does not mean your body is prepared. Only train physically with qualified in-person instruction, proper progressions, appropriate surfaces, safety equipment and spotters, and never attempt anything beyond your current, tested ability.

### New technology, no guaranteed results

V-Stim is a new, experimental and evolving method. It has not been clinically validated, it is not a proven training system, and it may change or be corrected as it develops. Individual results vary widely, and there is NO guarantee of any result — no guarantee that you will learn a skill, improve at it, lucid dream, or experience any effect at all. Any descriptions of how or why it may work are theory and personal experience, not established science.

### Not medical advice or treatment

LucidSkill is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Consult a physician before use if you have or have had epilepsy or seizures, migraines, a heart condition, an implanted pacemaker or other electronic medical device, a psychiatric, dissociative or psychotic condition, PTSD, a sleep disorder, a brain injury, or if you are pregnant. Do not use while under the influence of alcohol, cannabis, or any drug or medication that affects alertness, perception or seizure threshold.

### Sound & hearing safety

Sessions use binaural beats, isochronic tones, solid tones and voice tracks, often through headphones. Keep the volume at a comfortable, moderate level: extended listening at high volume can cause permanent hearing damage. Binaural and isochronic stimulation may also trigger discomfort, headaches or seizure activity in sensitive individuals — lower the volume or stop if anything feels wrong.

### Drowsiness, trance & re-orienting

You may become deeply relaxed, drowsy, or enter a trance-like or dream-like state, and you may fall asleep. Afterwards you can feel disoriented, groggy, or unsteady for several minutes. Give yourself time to fully re-orient before standing, driving, operating machinery, or making any decision that matters.

### Sleep & lucid dreaming

Night-time and lucid-dream practices can disrupt your sleep and may cause vivid, intense or distressing dreams, sleep paralysis, night-time awakenings, or daytime fatigue. Never use LucidSkill as a substitute for adequate sleep. If your sleep, mood or mental wellbeing is affected, stop and speak to a healthcare professional.

### Stop immediately if you feel unwell

End the session at once if you experience headache, eye strain, nausea, dizziness, chest discomfort, racing heart, anxiety, panic, confusion, unusual thoughts, numbness, or any involuntary movement. Do not push through discomfort, and do not resume until you have spoken with a doctor if symptoms were significant or recurring.

### Age & supervision

You must be 18 or older to use LucidSkill on your own, or have the informed permission and supervision of a parent or legal guardian. This app is not intended for young children.

### You accept the risk

You use LucidSkill entirely at your own risk. To the fullest extent permitted by law, Focuser LLC and its owners, employees and affiliates accept no liability for any injury, loss, damage or harm of any kind arising from your use of this app, its content, or your attempt to perform any skill — whether or not such harm was foreseeable. Always know your limits.
